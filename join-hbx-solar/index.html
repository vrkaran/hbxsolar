GOOD TO KNOW

A Few Quick Answers

Is this a Work From Home opportunity?+

Yes. Eligible candidates work remotely using calls and digital tools.

Do I need previous solar experience?+

No. Freshers and experienced candidates can apply, subject to selection.

How does the 30-day training work?+

Selected candidates learn solar basics, customer handling, sales and HBX Solar processes. Confirm training payment terms with recruitment before joining.

When does official employment begin?+

After meeting HBX Solar’s defined criteria and receiving employment confirmation. An application, training or first customer alone does not guarantee employment.

How is performance-based compensation calculated?+

Monthly slabs depend on verified successful customers and eligibility. Official employment terms define customer qualification, booking/payment requirements and incentives.
