Salary eligibility: 4 new customers

₹10,000 monthly salary requires 4 verified NEW customers in the monthly performance cycle, subject to applicable employment eligibility.

10 new customers: ₹23,000 total cash

At 10 verified NEW customers in the same monthly cycle: ₹10,000 salary + ₹12,000 performance bonus + ₹1,000 achievement bonus. This is total monthly cash earnings, not a ₹23,000 salary.

A fresh count every month

Customer count resets every month. Only verified NEW customers acquired during that month are counted. Official policy determines customer verification and booking/payment requirements.

Achievement Reward: additional

A separate Achievement Reward may be provided additionally when applicable. It is not included in the ₹23,000 total monthly cash earnings.

Earnings, bonuses and eligibility are subject to HBX Solar’s applicable performance and employment policies.

Yes. The compensation structure includes a monthly salary component along with performance-based bonuses, subject to applicable eligibility criteria.
