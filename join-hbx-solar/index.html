A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Your Questions, Answered

Is this a work-from-home opportunity?+

Yes. The program is designed around a Work From Home sales model for eligible candidates.

Do I need previous solar experience?+

No. Candidates can enter through the structured training program, subject to selection.

Is the training paid?+

Training payment terms have not yet been published on this page. Please confirm the current policy with HBX Solar’s recruitment team before joining training.

When does employment begin?+

Official employment begins after you meet the company’s defined employment and confirmation criteria, as set out in the official documentation.

What happens after my first customer?+

After your first successful customer and completion of the defined criteria, you can move into the official employment stage.

How is salary determined?+

Compensation is linked to verified customer acquisition and company-defined performance criteria. See the compensation structure above; the official employment terms govern eligibility, targets and payment.

Can I work from anywhere?+

The primary recruitment focus is Azamgarh District. Candidates from surrounding areas should confirm geographic eligibility with recruitment. Wider eligibility has not been confirmed.

What happens if I don’t acquire customers?+

The applicable performance review process and any initial support depend on company policy and your official terms. Ask recruitment to explain targets, review periods and the consequences of not meeting them before accepting the opportunity.

How are customers counted?+

Only acquisitions verified under HBX Solar’s official sales policy count. The precise qualification, booking and payment requirements must be confirmed in the official employment documentation; a lead or enquiry should not be assumed to count.

Can I grow into a leadership role?+

Yes, subject to sustained performance, capability, business requirements and company evaluation.
